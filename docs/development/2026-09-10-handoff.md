## Changes preserved

- Homing keeps normal holding current on waiting joints, restores normal current after referencing, fails on unmet positioning targets, and enforces a 60-second whole-sequence deadline. Positioning deadlines include planned travel plus settling time. A deadline that stops a failed run does not establish successful homing within one minute.
- Simulator driver feedback runs at the firmware cadence with encoder quantization and the velocity averaging window. Stream release retains braking until fresh encoder positions establish rest. Associated bus/stream regressions are included.
- Multicast startup probing uses an ephemeral receiver port so an existing subscriber cannot force unicast fallback.
- Collision-status reads use a separate latch lock rather than blocking on geometry queries.
- Calibration keeps the declared payload, holds position during sampling, requires stable fresh samples, and previews the approach excursions. This sampler still does not identify the physical load correctly under the simulated gearbox friction model.
- `config/PAR6.toml` contains an experimental J1 Kpv of 0.060, compared with vendor 0.015. It improved the tested simulator behavior but was not deployed or validated on the physical arm.
